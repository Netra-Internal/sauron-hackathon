How Sauron decides. Jev, our vision-language model, reads a four-frame strip every two seconds and scores nine facts. The policy turns those facts into an evidence score that builds and decays with a 60-second half-life. Thresholds: watch at 0.6, deploy at 1.0, urgent at 2.5. On the catalytic converter clip it reached 1.27 and deployed on the person under the car, with a caption the drone can use to find them.
