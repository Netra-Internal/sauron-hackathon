The problem

Site security doesn’t scale with people, and help arrives too late.

Guards

One post, one pair of eyes. Coverage costs more people, every night and weekend.

Remote monitoring

People watching walls of feeds. Most of what they see is nothing; the one thing that matters is easy to miss.

Response

Someone has to notice, call, and drive over. By then the lock is cut and the bike is gone.

Sauron watches every feed, decides by itself, and launches a drone. Operators only abort.
