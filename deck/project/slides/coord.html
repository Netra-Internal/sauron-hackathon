To send a drone to a person we need a map coordinate, not a pixel. The operator clicks a ground point in the frame and the same point on the map; with enough pairs we fit the camera’s pose. On the C920 test rig, 9 pairs give a median leave-one-out error of 6.6 metres, worst 21.7. That’s a desk rig, so treat it as a sanity check. Today we also fixed calibration for a camera that was never placed: from 6 or more pairs it finds the camera itself, and it recovered the surveyed pose with 7.2 m median leave-one-out.
