This is the whole chain we built today. Live camera feeds go to a GPU pod that boxes every person. The Sauron judge turns Jev’s facts into a verdict through the deployed policy. A calibrated camera plus sitefuse puts the person on the map. Then the drone gets a signed launch and a 1 Hz position stream. Launch is autonomous on a deploy verdict; the one-click Send drone is for the operator.
