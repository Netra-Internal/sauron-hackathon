04

The cameras stream over RTSP to the Jetson, which now only captures and pushes. We moved perception off the Jetson to a RunPod A5000 at 27 cents an hour today; cutover was 22:19 UTC. The pod runs detection, tracking and the live video; it posts through a relay on the droplet into netra-ask, which runs sites, sitefuse and dispatch. The /hack/ dashboard and droplink, our signed drone receiver, hang off the droplet. Pod inference: 34 to 46 ms per frame for the C920, 40 to 43 ms for the Axis, vs 75 to 86 ms on the Jetson.
